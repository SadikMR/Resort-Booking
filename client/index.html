You'll be delighted by the resorts $45 million modernization as you explore spectacular suites, artfully crafted lobbies, updated restaurants and bars, enhanced world-class spa and state-of-the-art fitness center. Sanctuary Cap Cana, A Luxury Collection Adult All-Inclusive Resort is located in Cap Cana, a private gated community retreat inside Punta Cana. Cap Cana is made up of 30,000 acres of flawless beaches, lush tropical gardens, and world-renowned golf courses designed by Jack Nicklaus and Punta Espada.

With 12 gourmet restaurants and 6 bars, including an overwater lounge and swim-up bar, the culinary experience is nothing short of extraordinary. Guests can indulge in cuisines ranging from authentic Dominican flavors to Mediterranean, Asian fusion, and contemporary French fine dining — all included in your stay.

The resort features seven sparkling pools, three rejuvenating whirlpools, a PADI-certified dive center, and a full-service marina. Whether you prefer lounging on the pristine white-sand beach, exploring vibrant coral reefs, or enjoying a round of golf overlooking the Caribbean Sea, Sanctuary Cap Cana offers an unparalleled luxury escape for discerning adult travelers.
